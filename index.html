Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. Coaching is financial education. We help you understand your money and build a plan, but we don't recommend specific investments or products.

If my employer sponsors me, can they see my results?

No. Your employer sees only anonymous group totals and how many sponsored sessions were used. Never your answers, score, finances or what you discuss with your coach.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions run online, so you can join from anywhere in the world. In-person sessions can be arranged on request.

What happens after my package ends?

You keep your Wealth Hub dashboard, monthly Financial Health checks and My Academy courses. You can book another package whenever you're ready.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes! We understand that unexpected commitments can arise. You can reschedule your coaching session by providing at least 24 hours' notice before your scheduled appointment.

Rescheduling is subject to availability. Cancellations or rescheduling requests made less than 24 hours before the session, as well as missed appointments without prior notice, may be treated as a completed session.
